MPA Digital Tidal Atlas hourly current predictions (set & drift) for 7 Singapore Strait points: .

Tide height: MPA high/low water predictions, Tanjong Pagar (published via NEA): . Outside these dates the app uses harmonics fitted to the same MPA data.

Copernicus Marine SMOC surface currents (Mercator 1/12° model: tide from FES + circulation incl. monsoon/wind + wave drift) for the Andaman Sea & Nicobar Islands, the Malacca Strait, Horsburgh and the South China Sea. Forecast window: —; outside it the app uses the model's tidal component only. Away from MPA points (beyond ~20 km) the map uses Copernicus; near them, MPA. The grid is ~9 km around Singapore and the southern Malacca Strait and ~28 km further out, so close inshore it can miss local detail.
